# Third-party models

- **Ships/Executioner** - "Ultimate Spaceships - May 2021" by Quaternius, CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-spaceships-pack
- **Sats/** - NASA 3D Resources (https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/, mirrored at https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources): Aqua, CALIPSO, CubeSat 2RU, GOES, ISS, James Webb Space Telescope, Juno. Used under NASA's media usage guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). The GLB files use Draco compression and need the glTFast + Draco packages.
- **Fish/** - "Animated Fish Pack" by Quaternius (Dolphin, Fish1-3, Manta ray, Shark, Whale), CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via https://opengameart.org/content/animated-fish. Source OBJ/MTL kept in `third_party/quaternius-fish/`; `tools/convert_fish.py` converts them to the JSON meshes here and also generates Tang, YellowTang, Needlefish and Puffer (original, same style).

## Nature (forest world)
Trees, bushes, grass and rocks in `Nature/` are converted from the "Lowpoly Nature Pack" by Quaternius (CC0): https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-nature-pack. Bird and Deer in `Animals/` are generated by `tools/convert_nature.py`.
